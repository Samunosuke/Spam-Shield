Spam Shield protects your Thunderbird mailbox against sophisticated spam, phishing, and disposable domain abuse using a multi-tiered DoH heuristics engine.
Traditional spam filters increasingly fail against automated disposable domains: Spammers register thousands of domains daily with seemingly valid SPF and DKIM records. Spam Shield detects these generator patterns through in-depth DNS and header analysis.
10-Tier Inspection Engine:
  1. Domain Existence: Verifies that the sender domain actually exists in DNS (no NXDOMAIN).
  2. DNSSEC Signature: Validates cryptographic authentication via DNSSEC (AD flag).
  3. MX Record: Verifies whether the sender domain publishes active mail exchange records.
  4. SPF Record: Checks for a valid v=spf1 TXT record in DNS.
  5. DKIM Signature & Key: Validates cryptographic email signatures and public keys (_domainkey).
  6. DMARC Policy: Detects missing policies or un-enforced p=none settings.
  7. Domain Age (RDAP): Identifies newly registered disposable domains (younger than e.g. 90 days) via the authoritative IANA bootstrap registry.
  8. Modular DNS Blacklists (DNSBL): Checks domains and connecting IPs against reputation lists (Nordspam, Spamhaus DBL, SpamCop, NiX Spam / manitu, 0spam, DroneBL), with customizable provider toggles and a configurable minimum hit threshold.
  9. SPF Sender IP Match: Validates the real sending MTA IP from Received headers against authorized SPF mechanisms.
  10. Spam-Kit Fingerprint: Detects automated domain templates (Self-MX 10 <domain>, raw-IP SPF, boilerplate DMARC, snowshoe subnets).
Key Features:
  • Native Thunderbird Context Menus: Scan entire folders or selected emails directly via right-click.
  • 1-Click Whitelist & Blacklist: Whitelist or blacklist individual senders or entire domains (with wildcard *.domain.com support).
  • Evaluation Mode (Dry-Run): Safely test and evaluate scoring without moving any emails.
  • Live Diagnostics Dashboard: In-depth hover tooltips for every check, log search/filters, and an interactive domain tester.
  • Privacy & Full Control: All penalty scores and thresholds are fully customizable. No third-party tracking — queries are executed directly and encrypted via DoH.
